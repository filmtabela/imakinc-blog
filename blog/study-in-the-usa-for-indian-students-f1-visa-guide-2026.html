F1 Visa Guide 2026: Study in USA for Indian Students

Planning to study in the USA in 2026? This complete F1 visa guide covers every step Indian students need, from university admission to visa interviews, with costs in INR.

F1 Visa Eligibility & Requirements for Indian Students

To apply for an F1 student visa, Indian students must secure admission from a SEVP-approved US university and receive a Form I-20. You must demonstrate strong academic records, English proficiency via TOEFL or IELTS, and sufficient financial proof showing at least ₹35–50 lakhs in your bank account to cover the first year of tuition and living expenses. A valid Indian passport with at least 6 months validity beyond your intended stay is also mandatory.

F1 Visa Application Process & Fees in INR

Start by paying the SEVIS fee of approximately ₹10,000 (USD 350) on the official FMJfee.gov portal, then complete the DS-160 online nonimmigrant visa application form. Schedule your visa interview at the nearest US Embassy or Consulate in cities like New Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai, Hyderabad, or Kolkata, and pay the MRV visa application fee of around ₹13,600 (USD 185). Carry your I-20, financial documents, admission letter, academic transcripts, and English test scores to your interview for the best chance of approval.

Total Cost of Studying in the USA for Indian Students

Tuition fees at US public universities range from ₹20–40 lakhs per year, while private universities can cost ₹35–70 lakhs annually. Living expenses including accommodation, food, and transportation add approximately ₹8–15 lakhs per year depending on the city, with New York and San Francisco being significantly more expensive than cities like Austin or Columbus. Indian students can reduce costs through graduate assistantships, scholarships like the Fulbright-Nehru Fellowship, and on-campus part-time work up to 20 hours per week permitted under F1 visa rules.

OPT, CPT & Work Rights After Graduation

F1 visa holders are eligible for Optional Practical Training (OPT), which allows you to work in the USA for 12 months after graduation in a field related to your degree. STEM graduates in fields like Computer Science, Engineering, and Data Science can apply for a 24-month OPT extension, giving up to 3 years of US work experience without needing an H-1B visa immediately. Curricular Practical Training (CPT) also allows internships during your studies, and many Indian students use this pathway to secure full-time job offers from top US companies before graduation.
