Australia Student Visa for Indian Students 2026: Complete Guide

Planning to study in Australia? Over 100,000 Indian students choose Australia annually. This comprehensive guide covers everything about the Australian student visa (subclass 500) process, requirements, costs, and timelines for 2026.

Australia Student Visa Requirements for Indian Students 2026

To apply for an Australian student visa (subclass 500), you need a valid Confirmation of Enrolment (CoE) from a registered Australian institution, proof of English proficiency (IELTS 5.5+ or equivalent), and Genuine Student (GS) requirement documentation. You must also provide academic transcripts, passport copies, health insurance (OSHC), and demonstrate financial capacity. Indian students must show funds of approximately ₹15-20 lakhs for tuition and living expenses for the first year.

Student Visa Application Process and Timeline

The application process begins with securing admission and paying your first tuition installment to receive the CoE. Apply online through ImmiAccount, upload all documents, pay the visa fee of AUD 710 (approximately ₹45,000), and complete biometrics at a VFS center in India. Processing times typically range from 4-8 weeks, though complex cases may take longer. Apply at least 12 weeks before your course starts to avoid delays.

Financial Requirements and Proof of Funds 2026

Indian students must demonstrate access to AUD 29,710 (₹18.5 lakhs) annually for living costs, plus full tuition fees for the first year. Acceptable proof includes bank statements showing consistent balance for 3-6 months, education loans from recognized Indian banks, or sponsor declarations with income tax returns. Your financial documents must be recent (not older than 3 months) and show genuine savings, not sudden large deposits.

Health Insurance, Medical Tests and Visa Conditions

Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC) is mandatory for the entire visa duration, costing approximately ₹35,000-45,000 annually. You'll need medical examinations from panel physicians in Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, or Chennai, and a police clearance certificate from your local police station. Once granted, your visa allows full-time study, up to 48 hours of work per fortnight during semesters, and unlimited work during breaks with potential pathways to permanent residency post-graduation.
