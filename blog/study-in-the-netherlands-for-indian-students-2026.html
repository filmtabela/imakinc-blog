Study in Netherlands for Indian Students 2026

The Netherlands is a top destination for Indian students in 2026, offering world-class universities, English-taught programs, and a vibrant international community. Discover everything you need to know about costs, visas, and top universities.

Top Universities in the Netherlands for Indian Students

Indian students can apply to renowned institutions like TU Delft, University of Amsterdam, Eindhoven University of Technology, and Wageningen University. These universities rank among the global top 200 and offer over 2,100 English-taught bachelor's and master's programs. Popular courses include Engineering, Data Science, Business, Agriculture, and Sustainable Development.

Tuition Fees and Cost of Living in INR

Tuition fees for non-EU international students typically range from INR 12 lakhs to INR 35 lakhs per year depending on the program and university. Monthly living expenses in cities like Amsterdam or Eindhoven average INR 80,000 to INR 1,20,000, covering rent, food, transport, and personal expenses. Scholarships like Holland Scholarship (up to INR 9 lakhs one-time grant) and Orange Tulip Scholarship can significantly reduce your financial burden.

Netherlands Student Visa Process for Indian Students

Indian students must apply for an MVV (Machtiging tot Voorlopig Verblijf) entry visa and a residence permit through their Dutch university, which acts as the sponsor with the IND (Immigration and Naturalisation Service). The process typically takes 4 to 8 weeks after university admission, so apply at least 3 months before your course start date. You will need proof of admission, financial sufficiency of approximately INR 9,000 to INR 10,000 per month, valid passport, and health insurance documents.

Work Opportunities and Post-Study Options in 2026

Indian students in the Netherlands can work up to 16 hours per week during studies and full-time during summer months, with average part-time earnings of INR 1,200 to INR 1,500 per hour. After graduation, the Orientation Year Permit (Zoekjaar) allows you to stay in the Netherlands for up to 12 months to find a job related to your degree. The Netherlands also offers a strong job market in tech, finance, logistics, and sustainability sectors with top employers like ASML, Philips, Shell, and ING actively hiring international graduates.
