Study in Dubai Without IELTS: Complete Guide for Indians 2024

Indian students can pursue higher education in Dubai without IELTS through alternative English proficiency tests, MOI certificates, or university-specific assessments. This guide covers affordable pathways to study in UAE.

Top Dubai Universities Accepting Students Without IELTS

Universities like Heriot-Watt Dubai, Manipal Academy Dubai, and Murdoch University Dubai accept Medium of Instruction (MOI) certificates from Indian students who completed education in English. Amity University Dubai and University of Wollongong Dubai also offer internal English tests. Annual tuition ranges from ₹4-12 lakhs depending on the program and institution.

Alternative English Proficiency Options for Indian Students

Instead of IELTS, students can submit MOI certificates from their Indian schools/colleges, take Duolingo English Test (₹4,000), or TOEFL (₹15,500). Many Dubai universities conduct their own English placement tests free of cost. Some institutions waive English requirements if you scored above 60% in English in Class 12 from CBSE/ICSE boards.

Admission Requirements and Documentation Process

Indian students need 10th and 12th mark sheets, passport copy, passport-size photographs, and academic transcripts for undergraduate programs. For postgraduate courses, bachelor's degree certificates and work experience letters are required. Most universities accept direct applications online, and processing takes 2-4 weeks with visa costs around ₹25,000-35,000.

Cost of Living and Scholarship Opportunities in Dubai

Monthly expenses for Indian students in Dubai range from ₹35,000-55,000 including accommodation, food, and transport. Student accommodation costs ₹20,000-40,000 monthly while sharing apartments reduces expenses. Universities like Middlesex University Dubai and SAE Institute offer merit scholarships of 25-50% tuition fee waiver for Indian students with strong academic records.
