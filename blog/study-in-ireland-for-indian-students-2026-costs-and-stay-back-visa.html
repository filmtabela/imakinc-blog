Study in Ireland 2026: Costs & Stay-Back Visa for Indians

Ireland is a top destination for Indian students in 2026, offering world-class universities, affordable tuition, and a generous stay-back visa. Discover costs in INR and post-study work options.

Tuition Fees in Ireland 2026 (INR Breakdown)

Tuition fees for Indian students in Ireland range from ₹12 lakhs to ₹35 lakhs per year depending on the university and course. Top universities like University College Dublin and Trinity College Dublin charge between ₹18–₹30 lakhs annually for postgraduate programs. Engineering, Data Science, and Business courses are among the most popular and competitively priced options.

Living Costs in Ireland for Indian Students

Indian students should budget approximately ₹8–₹12 lakhs per year for living expenses including accommodation, food, transport, and personal costs in cities like Dublin, Cork, or Galway. On-campus accommodation costs around ₹5–₹7 lakhs per year, while private rentals may go higher. Cooking at home and using student discounts can significantly reduce monthly expenses to around ₹50,000–₹70,000 INR.

Ireland Stay-Back Visa: Post-Study Work Options 2026

Ireland offers one of Europe's most attractive post-study work visas called the Third Level Graduate Scheme, allowing Indian students to stay back for 1 year after a bachelor's degree and 2 years after a master's or PhD. This visa lets you work full-time for any employer in Ireland, making it easier to gain international work experience and potentially transition to a long-term work permit. With Ireland's booming tech and pharma sectors, Indian graduates have strong employment prospects in companies like Google, Meta, and Pfizer.

Scholarships and Financial Aid for Indian Students in Ireland 2026

Several scholarships are available to reduce the financial burden for Indian students, including the Government of Ireland International Education Scholarship worth up to ₹16 lakhs covering tuition and living costs. Universities like University of Limerick and Dublin City University also offer merit-based scholarships ranging from ₹1.5–₹5 lakhs per year. Indian students are encouraged to apply early, as scholarship deadlines typically fall between January and March for the September 2026 intake.
