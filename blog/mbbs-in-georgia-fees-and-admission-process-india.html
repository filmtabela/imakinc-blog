MBBS in Georgia: Fees, Admission Process & Complete Guide 2024

Georgia has emerged as a top destination for Indian students pursuing MBBS abroad, offering WHO-recognized degrees at affordable costs. With no entrance exams and English-medium instruction, Georgian medical universities provide quality education starting from ₹3-5 lakhs per year.

Why Choose MBBS in Georgia for Indian Students?

Georgian medical universities are recognized by NMC, WHO, and ECFMG, making graduates eligible for FMGE/NExT to practice in India. The country offers European-standard education with affordable tuition fees ranging from ₹15-25 lakhs for the complete 6-year MBBS program. Indian students benefit from safe campuses, Indian food availability, and a well-established Indian community in cities like Tbilisi and Batumi.

MBBS in Georgia Fee Structure Breakdown (2024)

Total MBBS fees in Georgia range from ₹15-25 lakhs for 6 years, with annual tuition between ₹3-5 lakhs depending on the university. Top universities like Tbilisi State Medical University charge ₹4.5-5 lakhs yearly, while others like Batumi Shota Rustaveli State University cost around ₹3.5-4 lakhs annually. Additional expenses include hostel fees (₹80,000-₹1.5 lakhs/year), food (₹10,000-15,000/month), visa fees (₹25,000), and one-time admission charges (₹50,000-₹1 lakh).

Eligibility Criteria for MBBS Admission in Georgia

Indian students must have completed 10+2 with Physics, Chemistry, and Biology, scoring minimum 50% aggregate (40% for SC/ST/OBC). NEET qualification is mandatory for Indian students as per NMC guidelines, though no minimum NEET score is required by Georgian universities. Students must be at least 17 years old by December 31st of the admission year and hold a valid passport.

Step-by-Step MBBS Admission Process from India

Apply directly to Georgian universities with 10+2 marksheets, NEET scorecard, and passport copies between June-September for September intake. Universities issue invitation letters within 2-3 weeks, after which students apply for student visas at the Georgian Embassy in New Delhi. The complete process takes 4-6 weeks, and no entrance exams or donations are required. Students must carry attested documents, medical certificates, and obtain medical insurance upon arrival in Georgia.

Top NMC-Approved Medical Universities in Georgia

Tbilisi State Medical University (established 1918) is the oldest and most prestigious, charging ₹5 lakhs/year with excellent clinical exposure. Other top choices include Batumi Shota Rustaveli State University (₹4 lakhs/year), University of Georgia (₹4.5 lakhs/year), and Georgian National University SEU (₹4.2 lakhs/year). All universities offer English-medium MBBS programs with Indian food facilities and dedicated hostel accommodations for international students.

Living Costs and Accommodation in Georgia

Monthly living expenses in Georgia range from ₹15,000-25,000, making it highly affordable compared to Indian private medical colleges. University hostels cost ₹80,000-₹1.5 lakhs annually with basic amenities, while private apartments range from ₹12,000-20,000/month. Indian restaurants and grocery stores are readily available in major cities, with monthly food expenses around ₹10,000-12,000 for home-cooked meals.

FMGE/NExT Preparation and Return to India

Georgian medical universities provide FMGE/NExT coaching support to help students clear the licensing exam to practice in India. The pass percentage of Georgian MBBS graduates in FMGE ranges from 15-25%, similar to other international medical graduates. Universities include Indian clinical case studies in their curriculum and many offer dedicated FMGE preparatory classes in the final years to improve success rates.

Documents Required for MBBS Admission in Georgia

Essential documents include 10th and 12th mark sheets (notarized), NEET scorecard and admit card, passport valid for minimum 2 years, birth certificate, medical fitness certificate, and HIV test report. Students also need 10-12 passport-size photographs, bank statements showing financial capability (₹5-6 lakhs), and police clearance certificate. All documents must be apostilled from MEA (Ministry of External Affairs) India before submission.
