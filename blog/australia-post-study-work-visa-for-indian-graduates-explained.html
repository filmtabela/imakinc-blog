Australia Post-Study Work Visa Guide for Indian Students

Australia's Temporary Graduate Visa (subclass 485) lets Indian students work in Australia after completing their degree. Understand eligibility, costs, and how to apply successfully.

What Is the Subclass 485 Post-Study Work Visa?

The Temporary Graduate Visa (subclass 485) allows Indian graduates to live and work in Australia for 2 to 6 years after completing an eligible Australian qualification. It has two streams: the Graduate Work Stream for skilled occupations and the Post-Study Work Stream for higher education graduates. This visa gives you full work rights with no employer sponsorship required, making it one of the most flexible post-study options available globally.

Eligibility Criteria Indian Students Must Meet

To qualify, you must have studied in Australia on a valid student visa, completed at least 2 years of study in a registered Australian institution, and be under 50 years of age at the time of application. Your degree must be from an approved provider, and you must meet English language requirements with a minimum IELTS score of 6.0 overall. Indian students who completed a Bachelor's degree get 2 years, a Master's degree gets 3 years, and a Doctoral degree gets 4 years of work rights, with additional extensions possible for regional study.

Visa Costs and Application Fees in INR

The subclass 485 visa application fee is approximately AUD 1,895, which equals roughly INR 1,05,000 to INR 1,10,000 depending on current exchange rates. You must also pay for health insurance under the Overseas Student Health Cover (OSHC) extension and may need to pay for skills assessments if applying under the Graduate Work Stream, which can cost an additional INR 15,000 to INR 30,000. Budget an additional INR 10,000 to INR 20,000 for document notarization, police clearances, and medical examinations required during the application process.

Step-by-Step Application Process for Indian Graduates

You must apply for the subclass 485 visa within 6 months of receiving your final results, and the application is submitted entirely online through the ImmiAccount portal on the Australian Department of Home Affairs website. Key documents required include your Confirmation of Enrolment (CoE), transcripts, passport, proof of English proficiency, health insurance records, and a police clearance certificate from India issued by your local passport seva kendra or state police. Processing time typically ranges from 3 to 9 months, so apply as early as possible and ensure your student visa remains valid while the 485 visa is being processed to avoid any legal complications.
