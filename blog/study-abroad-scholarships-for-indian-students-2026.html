Study Abroad Scholarships for Indian Students 2026 Guide

Discover 50+ scholarships worth ₹5-50 lakhs for Indian students planning to study abroad in 2026. From government-funded programs to university merit awards, find funding opportunities across USA, UK, Canada, Australia, and Europe.

Top Government Scholarships for Indian Students 2026

The National Overseas Scholarship offers up to ₹20 lakhs annually for SC/ST students pursuing Master's and PhD abroad. Fulbright-Nehru Master's Fellowship covers full tuition plus ₹1.5 lakhs monthly stipend for studying in USA. Commonwealth Scholarship provides £12,000-15,000 (₹12-15 lakhs) annually for UK universities, with applications opening October 2025.

University-Specific Scholarships Worth ₹10-50 Lakhs

US universities like Stanford, MIT, and Harvard offer need-based aid covering ₹25-50 lakhs annually. UK institutions including Oxford's Reach Scholarship and Cambridge's India Trust provide ₹15-30 lakhs per year. Australian universities like University of Melbourne and ANU offer merit scholarships ranging ₹5-20 lakhs with automatic consideration upon admission.

Private and Corporate Scholarship Programs 2026

Tata Scholarship at Cornell covers full tuition (₹60+ lakhs over 4 years) for undergraduate Indian students. Inlaks Shivdasani Foundation provides up to ₹30 lakhs for postgraduate studies at top global universities. JN Tata Endowment offers loan scholarships of ₹10 lakhs at 4% interest for studying abroad, with applications accepted year-round.

Application Strategy and Eligibility Requirements

Start scholarship research 12-18 months before intended enrollment, as deadlines fall between October 2025-March 2026. Maintain 70%+ academic scores, prepare strong SOPs highlighting financial need and career goals, and secure 2-3 recommendation letters. Apply to 8-10 scholarships simultaneously to maximize chances, as competition rates range from 5-15% acceptance.
