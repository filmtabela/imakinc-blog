Study in Canada After 12th: Colleges & Costs for Indians

Canada is a top destination for Indian students after 12th, offering world-class education, post-study work permits, and PR pathways. Discover the best colleges, courses, and costs in INR to plan your Canadian dream.

Top Canadian Colleges for Indian Students After 12th

Leading institutions like University of Toronto, University of British Columbia, McGill University, and colleges like Seneca, Humber, and George Brown accept Indian students directly after Class 12. Diploma and undergraduate programs at community colleges are especially popular for their affordability and faster admission process. These colleges offer programs in business, IT, engineering, hospitality, and healthcare with strong industry connections.

Tuition Fees and Living Costs in INR

Undergraduate tuition at Canadian universities ranges from ₹12 lakhs to ₹35 lakhs per year, while diploma programs at community colleges cost between ₹7 lakhs and ₹15 lakhs annually. Living expenses including accommodation, food, and transport average ₹8 lakhs to ₹14 lakhs per year depending on the city. Toronto and Vancouver are costlier, while cities like Winnipeg, Halifax, and Hamilton are significantly more affordable for Indian students.

Eligibility and Admission Requirements

Indian students need a minimum of 60–75% marks in Class 12 (PCM or PCB or Commerce) depending on the program and institution. English proficiency scores like IELTS 6.0–6.5 or TOEFL 80+ are mandatory for most undergraduate and diploma programs. Some colleges also accept conditional admission and offer bridging English programs if your IELTS score is slightly below requirement.

Scholarships, Work Rights, and PR Benefits

Indian students can apply for scholarships like the Shastri Indo-Canadian Institute Fellowship, university merit awards, and provincial scholarships that can cover ₹1 lakh to ₹10 lakhs annually. Canadian student visa holders are allowed to work up to 24 hours per week off-campus, helping offset living costs significantly. After graduation, the Post-Graduation Work Permit (PGWP) allows students to work in Canada for up to 3 years, making it one of the strongest pathways to Canadian Permanent Residency (PR).
