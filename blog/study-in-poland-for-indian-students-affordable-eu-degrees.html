Study in Poland for Indian Students: Affordable EU Degrees

Poland offers EU-recognized degrees at just ₹3–8 lakhs per year, making it one of the most affordable study destinations for Indian students in Europe.

Why Indian Students Are Choosing Poland for Higher Education

Poland is a Schengen Area member offering globally recognized degrees, English-taught programs, and a safe, student-friendly environment. With over 400 public and private universities, cities like Warsaw, Krakow, and Wroclaw attract thousands of Indian students annually. A Polish degree is valid across all 27 EU countries, significantly boosting your international career prospects.

Tuition Fees and Cost of Living in INR

Tuition fees at Polish public universities range from ₹2.5 lakhs to ₹8 lakhs per year for international students, while private universities may charge ₹4–12 lakhs annually. Monthly living expenses, including rent, food, and transport, average ₹35,000–₹55,000 in major cities, which is far cheaper than the UK, Germany, or Australia. Total annual expenses including tuition typically fall between ₹7–15 lakhs, making Poland extremely budget-friendly.

Top Universities and Courses for Indian Students in Poland

Leading institutions include the University of Warsaw, AGH University of Science and Technology, Jagiellonian University, and Warsaw University of Technology, all offering English-medium programs. Popular courses among Indian students include Computer Science, Engineering, Medicine, MBA, and Data Science, with medical degrees starting at just ₹25–35 lakhs for the full 6-year program. Most universities have dedicated international student offices that assist with accommodation, visa paperwork, and cultural integration.

Visa Process, Scholarships, and Career Opportunities

Indian students require a National Type D visa for Poland, which requires an admission letter, proof of funds (approximately ₹6–8 lakhs in bank statements), health insurance, and accommodation proof, with processing typically taking 4–8 weeks. The Polish government and individual universities offer scholarships like the NAWA Banach Scholarship and university merit grants that can cover 30–100% of tuition fees. After graduation, students can apply for a temporary residence permit to work in Poland or travel freely across the Schengen Zone, opening doors to careers across Europe.
