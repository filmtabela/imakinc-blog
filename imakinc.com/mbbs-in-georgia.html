Overview

Why Study in Georgia?

Georgia has emerged as one of the most trusted MBBS destinations for Indian students, and for good reason. NMC-approved universities offer European-standard medical education at costs that are a fraction of what Indian private colleges charge — often ₹80–120 Lakhs total.

The entire MBBS programme in Georgia costs under ₹25 Lakhs total including tuition, accommodation, and living expenses across 6 years. Compare this to Indian private medical colleges where the capitation fee alone can exceed ₹50 Lakhs.

Tbilisi, the capital of Georgia, is a modern, safe, English-friendly city with a rapidly growing Indian student community. The climate is temperate, Indian food options are widely available, and the city is well-connected to India via direct flights.

IMAK Overseas has placed students at multiple NMC-approved universities in Georgia and provides end-to-end support — from application and visa to pre-departure briefings and post-arrival guidance. All counselling is free.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is MBBS in Georgia valid in India? +
Yes, provided the university is NMC-approved and you clear the FMGE/NExT exam after returning to India.
Is IELTS required for MBBS in Georgia? +
No. IELTS or TOEFL is not required for MBBS admission in Georgia. The entire programme is taught in English.
What is the total cost of MBBS in Georgia? +
Including tuition, accommodation, food, and travel, the typical total is ₹22–28 Lakhs for the full 6-year programme.
Is NEET mandatory for MBBS in Georgia? +
Yes. A valid NEET scorecard is mandatory for Indian students applying for any MBBS programme abroad, as per NMC regulations.
What is the duration of MBBS in Georgia? +
6 years total — 5 years of academic and clinical study, followed by 1 year of internship in affiliated hospitals.
