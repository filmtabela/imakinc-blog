Overview

Why Study in Dubai?

Dubai has emerged as one of the world's premier education hubs, home to branch campuses of globally ranked universities from the UK, Australia, Canada, and the USA. Students graduate with the same internationally recognised degree as the home campus — at significantly lower tuition costs.

For Indian students, Dubai offers unique advantages: it is just 3 hours by air from Hyderabad, making it the closest international study destination. With over 3.5 million Indian residents in the UAE, the cultural adjustment is minimal. Food, community, festivals — it feels familiar while giving you an international education experience.

Dubai operates as a tax-free economy — no income tax on part-time earnings or post-study salaries. The UAE's strategic location makes it a gateway for careers across the Gulf, Africa, and Asia. Students who stay back after graduation find strong employment opportunities in finance, technology, hospitality, and logistics.

IMAK Overseas helps Indian students navigate Dubai university admissions — university shortlisting, application, student visa, and pre-departure support — at zero cost to the student.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is IELTS required to study in Dubai? +
IELTS requirements vary by university and programme. Many Dubai universities accept a Medium of Instruction (MOI) letter from your school in place of IELTS for students from English-medium schools. IMAK will advise based on your specific university and course.
Is a Dubai degree recognised in India? +
Yes. Degrees from accredited UAE universities — including branch campuses of UK and Australian universities — are recognised by UGC and AIU in India. UK branch campuses award the same degree as the home UK campus.
Can Indian students work while studying in Dubai? +
Yes. International students in the UAE can work part-time with a work permit. Many students find part-time roles in retail, hospitality, and IT. Tax-free salaries mean 100% of earnings are retained.
How safe is Dubai for Indian students? +
Dubai is consistently ranked among the world's safest cities. With 3.5 million Indians in the UAE, there is a large and well-established Indian community, familiar food, temples, and cultural associations throughout the city.
What are the job prospects after studying in Dubai? +
Dubai is a major global business hub with strong demand for graduates in finance, technology, logistics, hospitality, and real estate. Tax-free salaries and proximity to India make it attractive for post-study careers. UAE offers post-study visa options for graduates.
