Why Study in the UK?

The UK is home to some of the world's oldest and most respected universities. For Indian students, a UK degree carries significant weight with employers in India, the Gulf, Australia, and globally. The 1-year master's degree structure means you spend less time and money compared to a 2-year programme in the US or Australia — and the Graduate Route visa lets you stay and work for 2 years after graduation.

🎓 World-Class Rankings

4 of the top 10 universities globally are in the UK. Oxford, Cambridge, Imperial College, and UCL consistently rank among the best in the world.

⏱ Shorter Degree Duration

UG in 3 years, PG in just 1 year. You save an entire year of fees and living costs compared to US or Australian equivalents.

💼 Graduate Route Visa

After graduation, you can stay and work in the UK for 2 years (3 years for PhD graduates) without needing a job offer beforehand.

🤝 Strong Indian Community

Over 70,000 Indian students study in the UK annually. Established support networks, Indian societies, and familiar food are available at most major university cities.

💰 Part-Time Work

International students on a Student Visa can work up to 20 hours per week during term time and full-time during vacations — helping offset living costs.

🌍 Gateway to Europe

Central location makes weekend travel across Europe easy and affordable. London especially is one of the most cosmopolitan cities in the world.

UK Student Visa (Student Route)

Indian students require a UK Student Visa to study at a licensed sponsor university. The process is well-established and straightforward if your documents are in order.

Requirement Details
CAS Number Confirmation of Acceptance for Studies — issued by your university after offer acceptance
English Proficiency IELTS Academic: 6.0–7.0 overall (varies by university and course)
Financial Proof 28 consecutive days of bank statements showing tuition + living costs
Visa Fee £363 (from outside UK)
IHS Surcharge £776 per year of study (NHS access fee)
Processing Time 3 weeks standard; up to 8 weeks during peak season
Dependants Permitted for postgraduate students (subject to conditions)
Post-Study Work (Graduate Route) 2 years after graduation; 3 years for PhD graduates

IMAK Tip

Chevening is the most prestigious and fully funded — but it's competitive and requires a strong professional profile. For most students, the combination of a GREAT Scholarship + part-time work + a non-London city is the most realistic path to making UK education financially viable. Talk to our counsellors to map the right strategy for your profile.
