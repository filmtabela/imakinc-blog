Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Is your counselling really free?
Yes, 100% free. We earn through partnerships with universities — they pay us a recruitment fee if a student joins through us. You never pay us anything for counselling, applications, or advice.
What if my grades aren't great?
Many of our partner colleges accept students with 50–60% aggregate. We have options for students with backlogs too, especially for private colleges in Singapore, Malaysia, and MBBS in several countries.
I don't have IELTS. Can I still study abroad?
Yes. Many partners don't require IELTS — especially Singapore, Malaysia, Dubai, and Malta. Some accept MOI (Medium of Instruction) certificates instead.
How long does the process take?
Singapore and Malaysia: 4–6 weeks. UK, Canada, Australia: 3–6 months. MBBS: 2–4 months. We'll give a realistic timeline at your first session.
