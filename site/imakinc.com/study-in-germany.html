Why Study in Germany?

Germany is the only major developed country that offers tuition-free education at public universities to international students, including Indians. Instead of tuition, students pay a nominal semester fee of €150–350 per semester — which typically covers a semester ticket for all public transport in the city. Total education cost for a 2-year Master's program in Germany can be under ₹25 lakhs all-inclusive, versus ₹80–120 lakhs for comparable programs in the UK, Australia, or Canada.

German universities — particularly the Technical University of Munich (TUM), RWTH Aachen, and Heidelberg — rank among the world's best in engineering, computer science, and natural sciences. A German degree is highly respected by employers in Europe, India, the Gulf, and globally. German industry giants including Siemens, BMW, SAP, Bosch, and BASF actively recruit from German universities.

After graduation, Germany offers an 18-month Job Seeker Visa — more than enough time to secure employment with a German or European company. Once employed, students can transition to a work visa and eventually apply for permanent residency.

The Germany equation: Tuition-free + Low living costs + 18-month job seeker visa + Strong engineering job market = One of the best value-for-money study destinations in the world — if you are willing to learn German or pursue an English-taught Master's.

Eligibility Criteria

  • Bachelor's degree (for Master's programs) — minimum 60–65% equivalent; GPA requirements vary by university
  • For English-taught Master's: IELTS 6.5 or TOEFL 90+ or equivalent
  • For German-taught programs: German language proficiency at B2 or C1 level (TestDaF, DSH, or Goethe-Zertifikat)
  • Statement of Purpose (Motivationsschreiben) and Letters of Recommendation
  • Blocked account with €11,904 (~₹11 Lakhs) — opened with Deutsche Bank, Fintiba, or Expatrio
  • Valid Indian passport and German National Visa (Category D — Student)
  • APS Certificate — mandatory for Indian students since 2022 (Akademische Prüfstelle verification of Indian academic certificates)

Admission & Visa Process

1
APS Certificate (India-Specific)
Apply for APS (Akademische Prüfstelle) certificate at the German Embassy in New Delhi. Mandatory for all Indian applicants. Submit academic documents for verification. Processing: 4–8 weeks.
2
University Application via Uni-Assist or Direct
Many German universities use Uni-Assist for international applications. Apply with Bachelor's transcripts, SOP, LORs, language certificates, and APS certificate. Deadlines: Jan 15 for summer; Jul 15 for winter semester.
3
Receive Admission Letter (Zulassung)
University issues an admission letter. This is needed for your blocked account application and student visa. Congratulations — at this point your degree is effectively tuition-free.
4
Open Blocked Account & Get Health Insurance
Deposit €11,904 into a blocked account (Fintiba, Expatrio, or Deutsche Bank) — accessible at €992/month once in Germany. Get student health insurance (TK, AOK). Both needed for visa.
5
Apply for German Student Visa
Apply at the German consulate in Hyderabad (VFS). Submit admission letter, APS, blocked account proof, health insurance, and SOP. Processing: 6–12 weeks. Book appointment well in advance.
6
Travel & Begin Studies
Fly to Germany. Register at the city municipality (Anmeldung) within 2 weeks of arrival — required for bank account, SIM card, and all admin. Enroll at university and collect semester transport pass.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to speak German to study in Germany?
For undergraduate programs, most require German at B2/C1 level since lectures are in German. However, hundreds of Master's programs — especially in engineering, computer science, and business — are taught entirely in English. If you want to study in English, go for a Master's program. IMAK Overseas will help you identify the right English-taught programs.
What is the APS certificate and why is it needed?
The APS (Akademische Prüfstelle) is a document verification service operated by the German Embassy in India. Since 2022, it is mandatory for all Indian applicants — universities will not consider applications without it. It verifies that your Indian academic certificates are genuine. Apply early as processing takes 4–8 weeks.
Is Germany good for IT/software engineers?
Excellent. Germany has a significant shortage of IT professionals and actively recruits from Indian universities and graduates. TUM, RWTH Aachen, and KIT have strong computer science programs with direct industry pipelines to German tech companies and European multinationals based in Germany.
Can I settle in Germany permanently?
Yes — Germany offers a clear pathway. After graduating and finding employment, you get a work visa. After 2 years of employment, you can apply for a settlement permit (Niederlassungserlaubnis). Germany's Skilled Immigration Act has made this process faster and more accessible for Indian professionals in STEM fields.
