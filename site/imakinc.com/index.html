How It Works

Simple. Stress-free. Sorted.

Most students go from first enquiry to offer letter in 4–8 weeks. Here's how.

01

Free Consultation

Tell us your academic background, budget, and goals. We assess your profile honestly and suggest the best-fit countries and universities.

02

Shortlist & Apply

We shortlist 3–5 universities that fit your profile, help you write your SOP, and submit applications on your behalf.

03

Offer & Acceptance

Once you receive your offer letter, we help you evaluate it, negotiate scholarships where possible, and confirm your seat.

04

Visa & Departure

We prepare your full visa documentation, guide you through the interview, and help you prepare for departure.
