Our Story

Started in Hyderabad,
Built for Indian Students

IMAK Overseas Education was founded with one clear frustration: too many students were getting wrong advice from consultants who prioritised commissions over careers. We built IMAK to be different.

We started by listening — to students who didn't have top scores, to families worried about money, to aspirations that didn't fit the standard "UK or USA" template. We built a consultancy around the full range of possibilities, from MBBS in Georgia at ₹3.5L per year to postgrad at top UK universities.

Led by Imran Khan, IMAK has supported 800+ students to study abroad over years of work in Hyderabad — from a full counselling team to today's focused, hands-on service. Students and families across India trust us for MBBS, UK, Canada, Singapore, and more.

No false promises. No pressure. Just honest, personalised guidance — whether you visit us in Secunderabad or speak with us on video from Punjab, Gujarat, or abroad.
