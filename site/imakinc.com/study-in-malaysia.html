Overview

Why Study in Malaysia?

Malaysia is one of the most affordable and culturally familiar study destinations for Indian students. With English as a primary language of instruction, globally recognised degrees, and tuition fees comparable to Indian private colleges, it delivers excellent value for money.

For CBSE and ICSE students, IELTS is not required at most Malaysian universities. A Medium of Instruction (MOI) letter from your school is sufficient. Students who hold a CBSE or ICSE qualification are automatically considered English proficient by many Malaysian institutions.

Malaysia has one of the largest Indian communities in Southeast Asia — Indian food, temples, cultural events, and Tamil-speaking communities are widespread in cities like Kuala Lumpur and Penang. The adjustment for Indian students is among the smoothest of any overseas destination.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I study in Malaysia without IELTS? +
Yes. CBSE, ICSE, or IGCSE students are generally exempt from IELTS at most Malaysian universities. A Medium of Instruction (MOI) letter from your school or college confirming English was the language of instruction is usually sufficient.
Is a Malaysia degree valid in India? +
Yes. Malaysian degrees from MQA-accredited universities are recognised internationally. Many Malaysian universities offer twinning programmes with UK, Australian, and US universities, further enhancing global recognition.
How much does it cost to live in Malaysia? +
Living costs in Malaysia are very affordable. Monthly expenses including accommodation, food, and transport typically range from Rs 25,000 to Rs 45,000 — far lower than Singapore, Australia, or the UK.
Can Indian students work while studying in Malaysia? +
Yes. International students can work up to 20 hours per week during semester holidays. On-campus work may also be available at some universities.
What is the visa process for Malaysia? +
The Student Pass for private universities is processed through EMGS (Education Malaysia Global Services). The university submits the application on your behalf. IMAK coordinates the entire process including the medical examination and documentation.
How does IMAK Overseas help? +
We provide completely free end-to-end support — university shortlisting, MOI letter guidance, application, EMGS visa coordination, pre-departure briefing, and post-arrival guidance. No hidden fees.
