Overview

Why Study in Singapore?

Singapore is Asia's most prestigious study destination — home to the National University of Singapore (NUS, ranked #8 globally) and Nanyang Technological University (NTU, ranked #15 globally). These institutions consistently outperform most UK and US universities in global rankings.

For Indian students from CBSE, ICSE, or English-medium state boards, IELTS is not mandatory at many institutions. A Medium of Instruction (MOI) letter from your school principal is sufficient for private colleges and waivable at NUS and NTU for students from English-medium institutions. Students who score 75%+ in English can often skip the test entirely.

Singapore is ranked one of the safest countries in the world — an important factor for Indian families. With over 25,000 multinational organisations headquartered there, including Google, Facebook, and JPMorgan, graduates have exceptional career placement opportunities in Asia and globally.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I study in Singapore without IELTS? +
Yes, if you studied at a CBSE, ICSE, or English-medium school. Most private colleges accept an MOI letter. NUS and NTU also waive IELTS for qualifying English-medium school graduates. Students who scored 75%+ in English in Class 12 generally qualify.
What grades are needed for NUS or NTU? +
NUS and NTU are competitive — typically 90–95% in Class 12. For private colleges like PSB, Kaplan, or JCU Singapore, 60–75% is generally sufficient. IMAK will assess your profile and recommend the best-fit institution.
Can Indian students work while studying in Singapore? +
Yes. Students enrolled full-time can work up to 16 hours per week during term time and full-time during holidays. Internships at MNCs headquartered in Singapore are commonly available.
How far is Singapore from India? +
Singapore is only 4–5 hours by direct flight from major Indian cities including Hyderabad, Chennai, Mumbai, and Delhi. This makes it one of the closest quality study destinations for Indian students.
Is Singapore safe for Indian students? +
Yes. Singapore consistently ranks among the top 10 safest countries globally. It has a large Indian diaspora, Indian food is widely available, and major Hindu temples and cultural events are common.
How does IMAK Overseas help? +
We provide completely free end-to-end support — university shortlisting, MOI letter guidance, application, Student Pass, pre-departure briefing, and post-arrival guidance. No hidden fees.
