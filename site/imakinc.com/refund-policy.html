Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: May 2026

Free counselling

Free calls and WhatsApp enquiries have no charge and no cancellation fee.

Study Abroad Fit Report (paid PDF)

  • Before we start work: Full refund if you cancel within 2 hours of payment and have not submitted the student details form.
  • After form submitted: No refund once we begin preparing your personalised report.
  • After delivery: No refund for change of mind. If the report was not delivered within 48 hours, contact us for a refund or redelivery.
  • Errors: If factual errors are our fault, we will correct and resend the report at no extra cost.

How to request a refund

WhatsApp or email with your payment reference (Instamojo receipt ID), name, and phone number. Refunds are processed to the original payment method within 7–10 business days where applicable.
