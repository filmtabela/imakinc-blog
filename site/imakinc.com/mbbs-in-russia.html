Overview

Why Study MBBS in Russia?

Russia has been one of the most popular destinations for Indian medical aspirants for over 30 years. With more than 70 state medical universities offering structured, high-quality MBBS programs, Russia combines world-class infrastructure with fees that are a fraction of private colleges in India.

Top institutions like Sechenov University, Pavlov University, RUDN University, and Lomonosov Moscow State University are accredited by NMC, WHO, WFME, ECFMG, and FAIMER — making the Russian MBBS degree valid for FMGE/NExT licensing in India, and recognized for USMLE and PLAB pathways abroad.

✅ Advantages

  • Fees from ₹2.5 lakh/year
  • No donation or capitation fee
  • NMC & WHO recognized degrees
  • English-medium programs available
  • 20,000+ Indian students currently enrolled
  • Strong clinical training (7:1 student-faculty ratio)
  • USMLE & PLAB pathways open
  • Government-subsidized education

⚠️ Considerations

  • FMGE pass rate varies by university
  • Russian language required in later years
  • Cold climate (especially Siberian cities)
  • Fees fixed in Rubles — exchange rate fluctuation
  • Limited direct flights post-2022

Eligibility

Eligibility Criteria for Indian Students

  • Must have qualified NEET UG (valid for 3 years from declaration date)
  • Minimum 50% aggregate in PCB in Class 12 (General category) / 40% for SC/ST/OBC
  • Age: Must be 17 years or older on or before 31 December of admission year
  • Must pass Class 12 from a recognized board (CBSE, State Boards, ISC)
  • Valid Indian passport at the time of application
  • No prior termination from any medical college on disciplinary grounds

Documentation

Documents Required

  • NEET scorecard (original + attested copy)
  • Class 10 & 12 mark sheets and passing certificates
  • Valid passport (minimum 18 months validity)
  • Birth certificate
  • Medical fitness certificate
  • HIV/AIDS test certificate (required by Russian authorities)
  • 6 passport-size photographs (white background)
  • Bank statement / financial proof
  • Police clearance certificate (some universities)
