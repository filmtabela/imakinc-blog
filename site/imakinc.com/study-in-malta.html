Overview

Why Study in Malta?

Malta is Europe's fastest-growing student destination for Indian students. As a full EU member state, degrees from Maltese universities are recognised across Europe and globally — yet the cost of studying here is far lower than the UK, Australia, or Canada.

One of Malta's biggest advantages is that IELTS is not required for most universities. Indian students from CBSE, ICSE, or any English-medium school can apply using a Medium of Instruction (MOI) letter. Entry is open after 10th standard for diploma programmes and after 12th for bachelor's degrees.

English is an official language of Malta — over 80% of the population speaks it fluently. The island is part of the Schengen Zone, meaning students can travel freely across 25+ European countries on their student visa. Students can also work up to 20 hours per week during term.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I study in Malta without IELTS? +
Yes. Most universities accept a Medium of Instruction (MOI) letter confirming English was the language of instruction in your school. CBSE and ICSE students can usually apply without IELTS.
Is a Malta degree valid in India? +
Yes. Malta is an EU member and its degrees are recognised under the European Qualifications Framework (EQF) and accepted globally by employers and postgraduate institutions.
What is the minimum qualification for Malta? +
10th pass is sufficient for diploma programmes. 12th pass (10+2) is required for bachelor's degree programmes at most Maltese universities.
Can Indian students work while studying in Malta? +
Yes. International students can work up to 20 hours per week during term time. This can offset Rs 3–5 Lakhs per year in living costs.
Can I travel to other EU countries from Malta? +
Yes. Malta is part of the Schengen Zone. With your student visa you can travel to 25+ European countries — Italy, France, Spain, Germany, and more.
How does IMAK Overseas help? +
We provide completely free end-to-end support — university shortlisting, MOI letter guidance, application, student visa, pre-departure briefing, and post-arrival guidance.
