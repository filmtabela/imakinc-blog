Questions

Who is this for?

Senior professionals, doctors, consultants, executives and founders, mostly in the UK, US, Europe, the Gulf, Canada and Australia, whose work depends on being trusted by people who look them up first.

How do I pay?

Monthly by international bank transfer in USD (GBP, EUR or AED on request). Invoices are issued by our billing company, IMAK Inc, Hyderabad, India, for FTech services. Launch Setup is billed 50% on start and 50% on delivery.

Do you need access to my accounts?

For LinkedIn, either you post content we prepare, or you give us limited access, whichever you're comfortable with. We never ask for banking or email passwords.

How soon will I see results?

Profile fixes show within days. Search results usually shift over four to eight weeks. AI assistants update on their own schedules, so we measure and report monthly rather than promise dates.

Who writes the content?

Our team drafts everything, using AI tools to work quickly, and every piece is edited and fact-checked before you see it. You approve it before anything is published.

Can I cancel?

Plans have a three-month minimum so the work has time to show results, then continue month to month. Cancel with 30 days' notice.
