About

A career built across international education, media and film, held together by one instinct: getting people from where they are to where they want to be.

Imran Ahmed Khan spent close to a decade marketing international education for Australian colleges across the Middle East and Southeast Asia. That work became IMAK Overseas Education — a Hyderabad consultancy placing Indian students in universities across the US, UK, Australia, Canada and Europe.

He started in film and theatre, and never left it. He writes feature screenplays, registered with the Screenwriters Association, and the storytelling shows up everywhere else — in how he interviews, how he presents, how he builds an audience.

Today he runs IMAK and writes.
